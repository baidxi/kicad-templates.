STM32 Nucleo-64 Development Boards

Expansion Board – Morpho Connectors

This project is a template of an expansion board for the "Zio" connector subset present on STM32 Nucleo-144 Boards.


This project includes a PCB edge defined to fit the four "Zio" connectors that are also compatible with Arduino shields. The connectors are aligned to ensure that the expansion board will fit onto the Nucleo-144. All IO on the Zio connector are present to the project through 0.1" expansion headers.

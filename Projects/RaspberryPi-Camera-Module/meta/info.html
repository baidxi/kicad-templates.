Raspberry Pi Camera Module 22 FPC

This project template sets up the basic structure for a camera sensor attached over MIPI and I2C

This template is sized to be compatible with other sensors, their optics and enclosures. Note that there is also a 15 FPC Camera module that exposes just 2 MIPI lanes and the 22 FPC can be reduced with a suitable adapter board.
